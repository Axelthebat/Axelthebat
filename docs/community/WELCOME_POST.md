## Welcome to the discussion board

I am Axel. I work on security tooling, Linux and networking, and cloud infrastructure. This is where I talk about what I build, document what I learn, and get feedback on my work.

### What belongs here

- **Q&A**: questions about a tool, a stack, or a security concept
- **Ideas**: feature requests and design proposals
- **Show and tell**: your homelab, tools, CTF write-ups, and network topologies
- **General**: security topics, networking, and everything else
- **Announcements**: releases and project news

### Security reports

If you find a vulnerability in one of my tools, do not post it here and do not open a public issue. Report it privately through [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Axelthebat/Axelthebat/blob/main/SECURITY.md). I acknowledge reports within 48 hours.

### House rules

- Search the board before posting. Most questions have an answer already.
- Redact secrets. No real credentials, keys, tokens, or production IPs.
- Bugs go to [issues](https://github.com/Axelthebat/Axelthebat/issues). Security reports go to the private channel above. Everything else belongs here.
- Keep it respectful.

### About me

Developer working across cybersecurity, infrastructure, and networking, mostly in Rust, Go, and Python. I document the process and welcome the feedback.
